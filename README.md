# The Lost Kingdom

**The Lost Kingdom** is a 3D adventure platformer game developed in Unity using C#.

The game takes place in a forgotten kingdom where a mysterious curse has taken away all light and life. The player takes the role of an adventurer whose mission is to explore the lost kingdom, overcome various challenges, and collect three magical crystals needed to break the curse and restore the kingdom.

The adventure takes place across three different environments:

- **Mystic Forest:** A mysterious forest filled with dangerous obstacles, poisonous mushrooms, and platforms. The player must explore the environment and collect the first magical crystal.
- **Ancient Cave:** An underground cave containing traps, moving platforms, and simple puzzles that challenge the player's skills and timing.
- **Lost Temple:** The final destination, where the player faces more difficult challenges and searches for the last magical crystal.

## Gameplay

The game combines exploration, platforming, and puzzle-solving elements. Players can move, jump, perform double jumps, collect crystals, avoid environmental hazards, and interact with different obstacles throughout the levels.

Each level introduces a different environment and challenges, making the adventure progressively more difficult.

## Objective

The main objective is to collect all three magical crystals, overcome the challenges in each level, and break the curse that has taken away the kingdom's light and life.
